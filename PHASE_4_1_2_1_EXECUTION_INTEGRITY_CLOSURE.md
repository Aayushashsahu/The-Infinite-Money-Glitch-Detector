## 1. Workstream Implementation Review

### Workstream A: Integrate Point-in-Time Availability Into the Actual Execution Path

1. **Explicit Point-in-Time Policy Modes (`PITMode`)**:
   Introduced the `PITMode` enum in `src/simulation/monte_carlo/schemas.py`:
   - `STRICT_VERIFIED`: Demands exact verified publication timestamps (`AvailabilityEvidence.EXACT_TIMESTAMP_VERIFIED`) with verifiable retrieved timestamps. If the dataset lacks verified timestamps (< 50 observations), the pipeline raises a clean, descriptive `ValueError` and refuses to claim strict verified point-in-time status.
   - `ESTIMATED_LAG`: Applies documented regulatory release schedules (ECB reference rates available ~16:00 CET; FRED US Treasury daily yields available next business day at ~15:00 UTC; central bank policy rates effective on announced effective dates). Retrospective observations are strictly excluded.
   - `RESEARCH_ONLY`: Preserves legacy research proxy semantics with full provenance tagging, admitting retrospective series as explicit research proxies.

2. **Strict Verified Data Sufficiency Guard**:
   An exhaustive inspection of `data/processed/gmdb.db` confirmed that out of 8,900 genuine historical observations, **0 records carry exact timestamp verification**, 8,724 observations use estimated release schedules, and 176 records are retrospective. Requesting `PITMode.STRICT_VERIFIED` immediately fails with a descriptive error informing the researcher that the database cannot support strict verified execution-order backtesting, preventing false claims of strict empirical validation.

3. **Release-Lag Alignment in `build_fx_carry_factor_panel()`**:
   For each daily trading session at European close (16:00 UTC):
   - Frankfurter / ECB reference exchange rate for date $T$ is published at ~16:00 CET and available at 16:00 UTC on date $T$.
   - US Treasury yields (`DGS1MO`, `DGS3MO`) for date $T$ are released by FRED on date $T+1$ at ~15:00 UTC. At 16:00 UTC on date $T$, the strategy only possesses Treasury yields for date $T-1$.
   - Implemented an $O(\log N)$ sorted `bisect` lookup indexing effective availability timestamps against session decision timestamps. The panel synchronizes exactly 990 calibration days (2020?2023) and 247 holdout days (2024) without look-ahead bias.

4. **Injected Factor Panel in `MonteCarloEngine`**:
   `MonteCarloEngine` now accepts an optional pre-built `panel: FactorPanel | None = None`. When passed, it directly uses the calibrated panel rather than rebuilding it with relaxed defaults. The panel's `pit_mode` is propagated to `MonteCarloResult` and `simulation_manifest.json`.

1. **Contractual Maturity Rollover (`rollover_mode="CONTRACTUAL_MATURITY"`)**:

## 5. Limitations & Scientific Disclosures

1. **Simulation Status**:
   All outputs represent conditional mathematical simulations under calibrated statistical distributions, not guarantees of future financial outcomes. The strategy remains classified as `SIMULATED_RESEARCH_PROXY`.
2. **Point-in-Time Reality**:
   Because the underlying database contains 0 exact timestamp-verified execution observations, the simulation study relies on documented release lag schedules (`ESTIMATED_LAG`). A genuine live execution system requires tick-level timestamp verification.
3. **Absence of Real Order-Book Frictions**:
   Frictions are modeled parametrically (30 bps bid-ask spread and financing spreads). Real market conditions may feature extreme spread widening and liquidity evaporation during systemic crises.
